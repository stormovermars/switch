# Case 13: a right invoked to deny a parent's role (m23), then a "yes" to the references (invented, 2026-11)

Toggles: none.

Invented test text, no real person, school or country. Goal: debate quality.

Bringing up a child is the state's job alone, and a parent who objects to the school board's new rule is endangering the child. Parents' convictions do not count when children's rights are at stake, so the board should refuse every parent a say from Monday, including a say in what their own child is taught about the rule.
