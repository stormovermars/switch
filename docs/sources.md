## The manifesto (principles 1-10)
Principles 1-10 are adapted (reworded) from the manifesto of the "Let's change the tone!" (Váltsunk hangnemet!) movement. Locator: the movement's Facebook post, https://www.facebook.com/valtsunkhangnemet/posts/pfbid0hBKBhQeFUB5vLgCQ9NPfTJgFXg72jqKoYzBCJTNtextTUEkm4uNGoYotL6h8kpasl . Its text is not included and not under the MIT licence; all rights stay with its authors. Principles 11-12 are our own.

## The jurists' statement (fairness points l01-l12)
The twelve points are our own checklist, informed by the statement "A jogállami büntető igazságszolgáltatás tíz pontja" (The ten points of a rule-of-law criminal justice system), Schiffer András and 31 co-signers, 29 October 2026, accessed 2026-11-01.
- original: https://www.facebook.com/schifferandras/posts/pfbid02Doiz6JF2oriePZ8mAaq3nH84qRmPKMymZ574WtRHWqP4gNjqrUf7K3PNteqcKHE8l
- full text: https://index.hu/belfold/2026/10/29/jogallamisag-nyilatkozat-miniszter-ugyved-alkotmenybiro/

Points 11-12 have no counterpart in the statement. The statement's text is not included and not under the MIT licence. Its authors have not reviewed or endorsed the checklist. The statement is a source, not a standard. (The decisions log of 2026-11-01 replaced the earlier wording "adapted from the ten points" with "informed by".)

## Frameworks
switch ships no framework data files and does not use the method frameworks (Admiralty, Toulmin, Walton, DISARM, First Draft and others) directly.

## Reference material
Reference copies (taxonomies, fact-checking datasets) are kept outside the repository in the maintainer's project folder. Only openly licensed material is kept; it is downloaded as untrusted data.
