# Public and private split

**Rule:** rules are free; data, and verdicts checked against data, are paid. The etiquette rules are always on. Every other feature is a lens (on / offered / off by topic) that the author can switch.

| Feature | Public switch | Paid edition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Etiquette: the twelve principles | always on | always on |
| Manipulation checklist (22 techniques) | lens | lens |
| Twelve fairness points l01-l12 and their ledger rows | lens | lens |
| Stage separation, national-code rule, jurists' statement offered as a reference | yes | yes |
| Press-ethics and law lenses | lens | lens |
| Instrument mapping per point | no | lens |
| National-law data and checks against it | no | lens |
| Press codes | no | lens |
| Evidence lake, registry, variants, specialist frameworks | no | yes |
| Licence | MIT, `NOTICE` for third-party texts | proprietary |

## Toggle rules
- The twelve principles are always on, as a whole and row by row. If the author asks to switch them off or marks a principle row "n/a", the skill says in one line that they always apply, reports the "n/a" and keeps checking.
- Every other lens is on when its topic is plain, offered when the topic is only touched, and off otherwise. The author can switch any of them; the choice holds for the conversation. A lens that is off is not run, not reported as a finding and does not drive a rewrite.
- A paid lens missing from the edition is listed as off, in one line, with no sales text. The free report carries no upgrade line; the paid edition is named only when the author asks.

## Where the paid parts live
The instrument mapping exists once, in the paid edition's private source, and is never copied by hand into this repository. The public history is kept as is (see [release.md](release.md)).
