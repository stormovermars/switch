# Lenses and toggles

**Rule:** the etiquette rules are always on. Every other feature is a lens (on / offered / off by topic) that the author can switch.

## Toggle rules
- The twelve principles are always on, as a whole and row by row. If the author asks to switch them off or marks a principle row "n/a", the skill says in one line that they always apply, reports the "n/a" and keeps checking.
- Every other lens is on when its topic is plain, offered when the topic is only touched, and off otherwise. The author can switch any of them; the choice holds for the conversation. A lens that is off is not run, not reported as a finding and does not drive a rewrite.
